Historic Square. Interstate Access. Room to Grow.

McDonough combines a lively 1820s town square with direct I-75 access and one of metro Atlanta's fastest growing job markets.

McDonough is the county seat of Henry County, about 28 miles southeast of downtown Atlanta on I-75. The town grew up around a traditional courthouse square that still anchors downtown, now lined with chef driven restaurants, a 1950s style diner, museums and seasonal events. Henry County has become a major hub for logistics, advanced manufacturing and healthcare, and much of McDonough's housing is relatively new. Renters get an easy commute, a younger community and a walkable downtown. Investors get strong rental demand, newer housing stock and steady job growth along the I-75 corridor.

  • 28 miles to downtown Atlanta
  • Direct I-75 access
  • Historic square founded in 1823
  • Median resident age of 31
