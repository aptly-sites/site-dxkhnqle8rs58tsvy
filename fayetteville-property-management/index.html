LIVING IN FAYETTEVILLE

Small Town Charm. Big City Momentum.

Fayetteville puts a historic downtown square, top rated schools and a booming job base just minutes from Atlanta's airport.

Fayetteville is the county seat of Fayette County, about 22 miles south of Atlanta. It has a walkable town square built around Georgia's oldest surviving courthouse. Trilith, the film studio campus and planned town where many Marvel films were made, brings creative jobs, dining and events to the area. QTS's huge new data center campus is drawing major tech investment. Renters get quality schools, parks, local restaurants and an easy commute. Investors get steady demand from a growing workforce in one of metro Atlanta's most desirable counties.

  • 22 miles to downtown Atlanta
  • Minutes to Hartsfield Jackson Airport
  • #4 school district in Georgia (Niche)
  • Home of Trilith Studios

THROUGH THE YEAR

Four seasons in Fayetteville.

Expect a mild winter, a green spring and a long stretch of summer warmth.

Winter

January average low–high

33–54°F

Cool, damp days with occasional frost. Snow is rare.

Spring

April average low–high

49–73°F

Mild days and new growth. The last frost usually comes around mid-March.

Summer

July average low–high

70–90°F

Hot, humid days often give way to afternoon thunderstorms.

Fall

September average low–high

66–84°F

September stays warm. The first frost usually arrives around mid-November.

About 52 inches of rain a year, spread fairly evenly across the seasons.
