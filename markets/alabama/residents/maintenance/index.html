RESIDENT RESOURCES

Maintenance help

For a routine repair, sign in to AppFolio and describe the issue. The team reviews requests during business hours in the order they arrive.

Submit a maintenance request ›

When to call

For fire, a medical emergency, or immediate danger, call 911. For urgent damage such as active flooding, call the maintenance line first, then submit a portal request.

Maintenance · (678) 782-7447

Who handles the repair?

Everyday resident care

Replace bulbs, detector batteries, and HVAC filters. Filters are due every 90 days. Report missed filter deliveries. Routine care also includes carpets, screens, cabinet hardware, lawn and bed upkeep, common pests, pet cleanup, and tissue clogs.

Repairs management coordinates

Report heating, cooling, plumbing, or electrical failures from normal use. Management also handles roof leaks, rotted wood, termites, exterior pressure washing, and large trees touching the house.

Damage-related costs

Residents may be charged for damage caused by wipes in plumbing, a water heater running empty, or damage from pets, guests, smoking, or misuse.
