Management & fees

What are your fees?

What we charge

  • 8% management fee on rent collected — we don't get paid until you get paid.
  • 1 week renewal fee — collected during renewal since we execute a whole new lease each year (we love long-term leases & leases that don’t end Nov–Jan 🙃).
  • Bi-Annual $42 Postage and Petties Fee — charged in January and July for HOA dues management (if applicable), utility management and billing, lawn care vacancy billing, maintenance programs, tax payment audit, insurance payment and audit.
  • $400 Owner Reserve — not a fee; funds held in your account monthly (visible in your owner portal).
  • Tenant Placement Fee: 100% of the first month’s rent if we fully procure your resident (marketing, photos, self-showing software, 3rd-party fraud-protected screening, 360° move-in report, and more).
  • Onboarding a tenanted property: $250.00 — includes contacting prior manager, system onboarding, transfer of deposit, etc.

What we don't charge for

  • Owner onboarding or off-boarding fee
  • Maintenance mark-up
  • Utility management fee
  • After-hours maintenance fee (we do have a 24/7 maintenance line)
  • Management of HOAs including dues, violations, etc.
  • Owner Portal management
  • & more (owners shouldn’t be nickel & dimed)

Management & fees

How do I get the rent money?

We use AppFolio for fast online rent collection and ACH disbursements.

  • Domestic clients: choose disbursement on the 10th or the last day of the month. If a resident pays after the 10th and funds clear, we can run another ACH.
  • International clients: disbursement on the last day of each month.

Note: ACH files are sent that day, but banking networks process overnight. You’ll receive deposits the next business day. Plan for Fridays/weekends accordingly.

If you pay a mortgage due by the 15th, keep funds set aside in case the draw or tenant payment is late.

Pet Policy

We evolved from “no pets” after recognizing most residents and our team have pets. With an estimated 66% of the population owning a pet (Jan 2024), we adopted a pet-friendly model.

Petscreening.com & All 3 Realty Pet Guarantee

Petscreening is a 3rd-party platform that scores and documents pets and ESAs, verifies ESA applications, and captures signed pet affidavits.

  1. Owner completes a pet application (per pet).
  2. Petscreening issues a FIDO score.
  3. Monthly pet rent based on score:

    • 4 Paws — $30/mo
    • 3 Paws — $40/mo

  4. Our Pet Guarantee: up to $2,500 in pet damages per year at move-out (e.g., door frames, fleas, carpet replacement).

Residents & screening

What are the rental screening criteria?

CREDIT

  • Use of CPN, recent credit repair, or new file (< 6 months) = automatic denial.
  • < 580 TU: requires co-signer/co-applicant with 620+.
  • 580–649 TU: conditional approval; must also pass income & rental history.
  • 650+ TU: sufficient for approval; must also pass income & rental history.
  • No score defaults to 400.

INCOME

  • Combined net income ≥ 3× rent.
  • Verification via RentEngine (paystubs/bank statements).
  • Self-employed: last filed tax return + 3 months bank statements.
  • SSI/Retirement allowed with proof of deposit.
  • Award/Offer letter: requires co-signer.
  • < 12 months employment: requires co-signer.
  • Spousal/Child support: verified by deposit (court orders alone not accepted).
  • Unemployment & Child Tax Credits are not income.
  • Active bankruptcy not allowed; discharged in last 5 years requires co-signer.
  • Pre-paying rent does not alter requirements.

RENTAL HISTORY

  • Most recent 2 years of good, verifiable rental/mortgage history.
  • Living with family/friends or subleasing is not verifiable history.
  • Unverifiable history (non-responsive landlord, no ownership proof, no ledger) is not accepted.
  • No verifiable history: must meet minimum Income (3× net) and Credit (650+); additional deposit may be required.
  • Applicants must have given notice to current landlord per lease terms.
  • Poor current landlord history (balances, 2+ late pays/year, violations) may result in rejection.

Residents & screening

Do I need a Co-Signer?

If “Yes” to any of the below, you need a co-signer:

  • No verifiable rental history (living with family/friends or subleasing)
  • Credit score < 580 (TransUnion)
  • Newly employed with only Offer/Award Letter
  • Employed < 90 days
  • Rental collection > $1,000 in last 5 years
  • Discharged bankruptcy in last 5 years

Co-Signer Requirements

  • Minimum 650 credit score
  • Income ≥ 2× rent (combined for approval)
  • Verifiable mortgage or rental history in co-signer’s name
  • Co-signers sign the lease and are financially responsible

Residents & screening

What happens if my tenant doesn't pay rent?

  • Rent due on the 1st; late at 12:00 AM on the 6th (after midnight on the 5th).
  • Resident has until 5 PM on the 15th to provide a Promise to Pay date.
  • We review history (lateness, home condition, prior promises) to decide next steps.
  • If not paid in full (with fees) by the 4th Friday, we initiate eviction.
  • Final notice posted: 7 business days to pay or we file.
  • Court operations have resumed post-COVID.

Current Owner, Move Out Statements

Charges (management fee, RBP, etc.) may appear after move-out due to AppFolio workflows:

  1. Security Deposit (SD) moves from SD bank to Rent Escrow — appears on your statement.
  2. Refunded SD issues a check to the tenant (e.g., $995), reducing balance.
  3. If unpaid charges exist, AppFolio applies SD to oldest charges (e.g., rent income, late fee, RBP).
  4. When rent income is recognized, AppFolio assesses the 8% management fee (and applicable late/RBP fees).

This is why those fees appear on the statement.
