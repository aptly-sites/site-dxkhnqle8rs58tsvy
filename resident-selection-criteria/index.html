FUTURE RESIDENTS

Application Criteria

Before you apply, review the factors All 3 Realty considers when evaluating an application. These include credit, income, rental history, and any co-signer requirements.

Application process

All 3 Realty welcomes applicants of every race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, and national origin in accordance with federal fair housing law.

  1. Check availability. Before applying, text 678-782-7447 to confirm availability and ask how many applications are pending. Marketing continues until approval and payment of hold money.
  2. Submit your application. Each prospective occupant over 18 must apply separately, upload all required documents at submission, and pay a nonrefundable $70 fee. Missing documents make an application incomplete. Withdrawing before completion cancels the application without a refund or further obligations.
  3. Watch for a decision. RentEngine typically processes applications in 1–4 business days with complete documents and prompt landlord verification. We’ll contact you immediately with the decision. Approvals remain on file for 60 days. If the home becomes unavailable, we can transfer an approval to another home or share matching upcoming rentals.
  4. Reserve your home. After approval, pay the first month’s rent in full plus a $150 administrative fee. This hold money is nonrefundable. Within 15 days of payment, take possession and pay the full security deposit. This deadline cannot be extended. The deposit usually equals one month’s rent. Conditional approval may require more.

Credit

  • Use of a CPN #, Credit Repair and/or a New Credit File that has started in the last 6 months will result in an Automatic Denial
  • Transunion Credit Scores under 580 must be accompanied by a Co-Signer or Co-Applicant with a credit score that exceeds a 620+
  • Transunion credit scores between 580-649 can be approved conditionally and must pass the income and rental history requirements
  • Transunion Credit Scores 650+ or higher are sufficient for approval and also must pass the income and rental history requirements
  • If you do not have a Credit Score, it will automatically default to a 400  

Income

  • Applicants must have a COMBINED Net Income (After taxes) of at least 3 x the rental rate
  • Paystubs and bank statements are verified through a 3rd party company, RentEngine to ensure accuracy and authenticity
  • Applicants who are self-employed must provide filed tax returns of the previous fiscal year & 3 months of bank statements
  • Additional income such as SSI and Retirement benefits are accepted as long as proof of deposit is provided
  • Applicants with an Award Letter or an Offer letter MUST have a Co-Signer
  • Applicants who have been employed less than 12 months MUST have a Co-signer
  • Spousal Support and Child Support must be verified by the actual DEPOSIT of these funds. Court Ordered documents will not be considered.
  • Unemployment and Child Tax Credits are NOT considered income
  • Applicants who are in an ACTIVE bankruptcy will not be considered for a rental with All 3 Realty, Bankruptcies much be discharged. Applicants MUST have a Co-Signer if it has been discharged in the last 5 years.
  • Pre-paying rent does NOT alter our rental requirements

Rental history

  • 2 MOST RECENT Years of good, VERIFIABLE, rental or mortgage history (Late payments and poor reference will affect the approval)
  • Living With family or a friend and/or Subleasing is not accepted as verifiable rental history
  • Unverifiable rental history including non-responsive landlords, landlords who cannot provide property ownership verification and lack of rental payment ledger will be scored will not be considered verifiable rental history.
  • If you do not have verifiable rental history and/or have been living with family/subleasing, You MUST meet our minimum Income (3 x Net) and Credit (650+) requirements, Additional Deposit may be required
  • Applications must have given Property Notice to Vacate to their current landlord per their lease terms with their current management company
  • Poor rental history from your current landlord will result in rejection (current balances, poor payment history (2+ late pays per year), lease violations, etc)

When you need a co-signer

  • I have no verifiable rental history ( I have been living with family, friends or subleasing)
  • I have a credit score under 580 (TransUnion)
  • I am newly employed and can only provide an Offer or Award Letter
  • I have been employed at my company for less than 90 days
  • I have a rental collection in the last 5 years that exceeds $1000.00
  • I have had a DISCHARGED bankruptcy in the last 5 years

Co-signer requirements

  • Must have a minimum 650 Credit Score
  • Must have 2x the rent income (your incomes will be combined for approval)
  • Must have VERIFIABLE mortgage or rental history in the Co-Signers name
  • Co-signs are required to sign the lease & are a financially responsible party
