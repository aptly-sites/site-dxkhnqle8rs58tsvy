Living in Prattville.

Founded in 1839 by industrialist Daniel Pratt, Prattville is known as "The Fountain City" for the artesian wells that still flow through town. Today it blends a walkable historic downtown along Autauga Creek with modern shopping, dining and neighborhoods on the east side near I-65. Renters enjoy creekside trails, community events and the Robert Trent Jones Golf Trail at Capitol Hill, all with quick access to jobs across the Montgomery metro. Investors get a stable, higher income community where most residents own their homes, which keeps quality rentals in steady demand.

  • "The Fountain City"
  • 54 holes on the Robert Trent Jones Golf Trail
  • About 10 miles from Montgomery
  • Median household income of $81,337

Weather through the year.

Prattville shares the Montgomery area's humid subtropical climate: short, mild winters, long hot summers, and pleasant springs and falls. The area gets about 53 inches of rain a year, and October is usually the driest month.
